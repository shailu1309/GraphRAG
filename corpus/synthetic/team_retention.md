# Retention - Team Page

## Who we are

The Retention team handles customers who have asked to cancel, downgrade or port out, and tries to keep them where that makes sense for both sides. Aisha Rahman leads the team. Day-to-day floor questions go to the duty supervisor first. Escalate to Aisha only if the supervisor can't resolve it or it involves policy.

We are not a sales team. Our job is to find out why the customer wants to leave, fix the underlying problem if we can, and offer something proportionate if we can't. If the customer still wants to go after that, we process the cancellation cleanly and politely. A customer who leaves on good terms is more likely to come back.

## What we handle

The Retention team answers the SAVE queue. Calls and chats land there when a customer selects the cancellation option in the IVR, or when another team transfers them after the customer says they want to leave.

Typical contacts:

- Cancellation requests at or near the end of a minimum term
- Customers quoting a competitor price
- Complaints about speed, outages or billing that have tipped into "I'm leaving"
- Downgrade requests where the customer wants to cut cost but keep service
- Port-out requests on mobile

Not ours, so transfer these:

- Bereavement and power-of-attorney cases go to the specialist accounts team.
- Active fault investigations go to technical support. Note the cancellation intent on the account first.
- Disputed debt goes to the collections team.

Before transferring, always leave a note on the account so the customer doesn't have to repeat themselves.

## Win-back campaign

Aisha Rahman runs the Win-back campaign. This is our outbound activity aimed at customers who have already left. The campaign pulls lapsed-customer lists from the Relay CRM. Agents then work through those lists during scheduled outbound blocks.

Rules for Win-back calls:

1. Check the do-not-contact flag before every dial, even if the list was pulled that morning.
2. Read the leaving reason on the account before you call. Don't open with an offer if they left over a fault we never fixed.
3. Log every outcome: no answer, declined, callback, or won back.
4. Two failed attempts and the record is closed for this cycle.
5. Offers must come from the current approved offer sheet only. No improvising.

List quality issues go to Aisha, not the duty supervisor. Raise duplicates, customers who have already rejoined, or missing contact details.

## Offers and authority

| Offer type | Agent | Supervisor approval |
|---|---|---|
| Standard retention discount (offer sheet) | Yes | No |
| Free speed upgrade for remaining term | Yes | No |
| Early termination fee waiver | No | Yes |
| Bill credit over the agent limit | No | Yes |

Record the offer made and the reason on every save, whether or not the customer accepts. The finance team audits retention discounts monthly. An offer with no recorded reason will be queried.

## Team rhythm

- **Daily huddle:** 10 minutes at shift start. Covers queue forecast, offer sheet changes and any known outages.
- **Weekly review:** Thursday afternoon. Covers save rate, Win-back results and two or three call recordings, good and bad.
- **Monthly one-to-ones:** with your supervisor.
- **Open actions:**
  - Refresh the offer sheet ahead of next quarter's pricing change. Owner: Aisha Rahman.
  - Update the transfer notes template. Owner: duty supervisor.
  - Agents to complete the refreshed complaint-handling module by month end.

New starters shadow the SAVE queue for their first week before taking contacts alone. They don't join Win-back blocks until they have been signed off on the offer sheet.
