# Billing Support - Team Page

Last reviewed by the team lead. If something here is out of date, flag it in the team channel rather than editing around it.

## Who we are

Dan Reyes leads the Billing Support team. We handle customer contacts about charges, payments, refunds, credits and anything else that shows up on a bill and doesn't look right to the customer. We are a mix of full-time and part-time agents across two shifts, with a supervisor on each shift for escalations and approvals.

We are not the collections team and we are not technical support. If a customer's real problem is a fault or an outage, warm-transfer them. Don't try to troubleshoot it from the billing side.

## What we cover

Billing Support agents answer the BILL queue. Typical contacts include:

- Explaining a first bill, including part-month charges after a new connection or plan change
- Disputed charges, such as roaming, premium numbers, add-ons and early termination fees
- Failed or duplicate payments, and updating payment details
- Applying goodwill credits within your approval limit
- Raising refund requests and setting expectations on timescales
- Changing bill delivery, billing date or paper/email preference

Anything above your credit limit goes to the shift supervisor. Suspected fraud or account takeover goes straight to the supervisor, and the call should not be handled further until they've reviewed it. Complaints that a customer wants formally logged follow the standard complaints process. Note the case reference on the account before closing.

## How we work

**Shifts and breaks.** Early and late shifts overlap for one hour at midday so handovers can happen live. Breaks are staggered by the supervisor at the start of each shift. Please don't swap without asking, because queue cover drops fast.

**Meetings.**
- Daily huddle, 10 minutes at shift start: queue volumes, known issues, any billing run problems.
- Weekly team meeting, 30 minutes on Thursdays: case reviews, process changes, Q&A with the team lead.
- Monthly one-to-ones with your supervisor.

**Notes standard.** Every contact gets an account note covering what the customer asked, what you checked, what you did, and what happens next with a date. Write notes so the next agent doesn't have to ask the customer to repeat themselves.

**Credits and refunds.** Always quote the amount and the bill it will appear on. Never promise a refund date tighter than the published timescale.

## Targets and quality

We aim to answer most contacts within the service level set for the queue and to resolve them on first contact wherever possible. Quality reviews sample a handful of contacts per agent each month and score accuracy, notes, compliance checks and tone. Accuracy matters more than speed. A fast wrong answer generates a repeat contact and often a complaint.

Common review fails:
1. Skipping security checks before discussing the account
2. Applying a credit without noting the reason
3. Telling the customer a charge is "definitely an error" before checking

## New starters and contacts

New agents shadow an experienced colleague for their first week, then take live contacts with a buddy listening in for the second week. Your supervisor will sign off your credit limit once you've passed the billing knowledge check.
