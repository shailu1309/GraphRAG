# Technical Support - Team Page

## Who We Are

Technical Support handles fault diagnosis and resolution for broadband and mobile customers. The team is split into two tiers, with additional overflow capacity provided by an outsourced partner. This page covers the basics for new starters, colleagues in other departments, and anyone who needs to know where a technical contact goes.

Please keep this page current. If something here is wrong, tell a team supervisor rather than working around it.

## Structure and Queues

**Tier 1 Technical Support** answers the **TECH-1** queue. This is the first point of contact for customers calling with technical issues. Typical contacts include:

- Loss of service or intermittent connection
- Slow speeds and Wi-Fi coverage complaints
- Router setup and replacement queries
- Mobile data, signal and SIM issues
- Basic account checks before troubleshooting

**Telvio**, an outsourced partner, takes overflow calls from the **TECH-1** queue. Customers should get the same standard of handling whichever agent picks up. If you notice a pattern in contacts that have passed through overflow, such as repeat calls or incomplete notes, raise it with a supervisor with example references. Do not contact partner agents directly about individual cases.

**Tier 2 Technical Support** is led by **Luis Ortega**. Tier 2 deals with more complex or persistent faults that need deeper investigation, longer diagnostic time, or coordination with field and network teams.

## How We Work

**Shift pattern:** Rotas are published four weeks in advance. Swap requests go to a supervisor at least five working days before the shift.

**Meetings:**
- Daily stand-up, 10 minutes at the start of the morning shift. Covers overnight issues, known outages and staffing.
- Weekly team meeting, 30 minutes on Wednesday afternoons. Covers quality scores, recurring faults and process changes.
- Monthly calibration session, where a sample of calls is scored together to keep quality assessments consistent.

**Case notes:** Every contact needs a note that someone else could pick up cold. At minimum, record:
1. The customer's reported symptom, in their words where possible
2. The checks completed and their results
3. What was changed, if anything
4. The agreed next step and timescale
5. Whether the customer was told about any follow-up

**Known outages:** Before troubleshooting, check the current outage notices. If the customer is in an affected area, confirm this, give the latest update, and avoid unnecessary home equipment checks.

## Escalation and Handover Checklist

Before passing a case on, confirm:

- [ ] The customer's identity has been verified
- [ ] Basic troubleshooting has been completed and noted
- [ ] The fault has been reproduced or clearly described
- [ ] Any reference numbers have been given to the customer
- [ ] The customer knows the expected response time
- [ ] Vulnerability or accessibility needs are flagged on the account

Incomplete handovers will be returned. This is not a judgement. It saves the customer a repeat call.
