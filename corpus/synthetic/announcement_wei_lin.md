# Announcement: New Network Escalations Lead

**Distribution:** All contact centre staff, team leaders and duty managers
**Effective:** January 2026
**Issued by:** Contact Centre Operations

## Summary

We are pleased to confirm that **Wei Lin** was promoted to lead **Network Escalations** in January 2026. This note covers Wei's background, what changes for frontline and technical teams, and the practical points you need to know during the handover period.

Please read the whole note, particularly if you raise or receive escalations as part of your role.

## Background

Before this promotion, Wei was a senior agent in **Tier 2 Technical Support**. In that role Wei handled complex fault cases and acted as an informal point of contact for colleagues working through difficult diagnostics. Wei also coached newer agents on structured fault logging and supported shift leaders during periods of high call volume.

Many of you will already have worked alongside Wei. Those who haven't will find Wei approachable and very direct about what information an escalation needs before it can move forward.

## What Changes

For most staff, day-to-day processes stay the same. The main changes are:

- **Point of contact.** Wei is now the first named contact for questions about how escalations into Network Escalations are prioritised and progressed. Routine case updates should still go through the usual channels and should not be sent to Wei directly.
- **Escalation quality.** Wei has asked that all escalations include the standard minimum details: customer account reference, service type, fault start time, troubleshooting steps already completed, and any customer-reported pattern (time of day, weather, specific devices). Incomplete escalations may be returned to the originating agent.
- **Weekly review.** A 30-minute escalation review will run every Thursday. Team leaders from frontline and technical teams are invited to attend, or to send a delegate. A calendar invite will follow.
- **Monthly summary.** From the end of February, a short monthly summary of escalation volumes, common fault themes and turnaround times will be circulated to team leaders.

Tier 2 Technical Support will backfill Wei's former senior agent position. Details of that process will be shared separately by the relevant manager.

## Handover Period

During the first weeks of the new role, please allow for some adjustment:

1. Open escalations raised before the change will be completed under the existing arrangements. Do not re-raise them.
2. If you are unsure whether a case meets the threshold for escalation, check with your team leader first.
3. Any feedback on the escalation process, positive or otherwise, can be logged through the usual team leader feedback route. Wei has said all comments will be read during the first review cycle.
4. Team leaders should make sure their teams have read this announcement by the end of the week.

## Next Steps

- **Team leaders:** confirm attendance or a delegate for the Thursday review.
- **All agents:** review the minimum escalation details listed above and apply them from today.
- **Duty managers:** update any local contact sheets that list escalation contacts.

Please join us in congratulating Wei on the new role. Questions about this announcement should be directed to your team leader in the first instance.

*Contact Centre Operations*
