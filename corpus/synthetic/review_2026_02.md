# Customer Operations Monthly Review - February 2026

## Summary

The February review was chaired by M. Okafor, Head of Customer Operations. Attendees included operations managers, team supervisors, the workforce planning lead, and a representative from the quality team. Apologies were received from the training team, who will be sent the notes.

February was mixed. Overall contact volumes were broadly in line with forecast. Two issues dominated the month: out-of-date knowledge content for the new router, and poor cover on Monday mornings. Both are covered below with actions.

## Service Performance

The contact centre was understaffed on Monday mornings throughout February, and the service level fell to 61% during those periods. Outside Monday mornings, performance was close to target. However, the Monday shortfall pulled the monthly average down and generated a noticeable rise in abandoned calls and callbacks later in the week.

Workforce planning noted three likely causes:

- Monday morning demand has risen since the start of the year, but the rota has not been updated to match.
- Unplanned absence was higher on Mondays than on other weekdays.
- Training sessions were booked into early-week slots, which took agents off the phones at peak times.

Supervisors reported that queues built up quickly before mid-morning and did not fully recover until after lunch. Customers who waited longest were more likely to raise a complaint, and the complaints handlers have seen some of this carry through into their workload.

## Knowledge and Quality

In February, TECH-1 agents reported that Beacon articles about the new router were out of date. As a result, customers received setup and troubleshooting steps that no longer matched their equipment and had to call back. The quality team's call sampling confirmed the pattern. Several sampled repeat contacts followed an earlier call where the agent had followed the published article correctly, but the article itself was wrong.

The discussion covered these points:

- Agents had raised the problem informally with supervisors before it was logged. Feedback needs a clearer route so content issues are escalated sooner.
- Articles for new equipment should be reviewed before launch and again within a few weeks of go-live.
- Until the articles are corrected, supervisors will brief agents on the known differences at the start of each shift.

M. Okafor asked that repeat-contact rates for router-related calls be tracked separately in March. This will show whether the corrections are working.
