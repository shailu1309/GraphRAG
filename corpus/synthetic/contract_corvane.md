# Vendor Contract Summary: Corvane Systems

**Owner:** CX Platforms (lead: Grace Kim)
**Document type:** Internal contract summary, for contact centre management and finance review
**Status:** Current. Review due before next renewal window.

## Contract Overview

Corvane Systems supplies the Relay CRM used by the customer contact centre. CX Platforms owns the vendor contracts for contact centre tooling, including this one. Grace Kim leads CX Platforms and is the accountable owner for the commercial relationship, renewal decisions and escalation to the vendor.

The contract covers:

- Named-user seat licences for Relay CRM
- Standard support, with tiered response targets by severity
- Hosting, maintenance releases and security patching
- Access to a sandbox environment for testing configuration changes

Not covered under the base agreement:

- Custom integration work, which is quoted separately as statements of work
- Additional seats above the contracted volume, which are billed as overage
- Premium support hours outside the standard window

## Commercial Terms and Spend

Seats are priced per named user per month against a contracted baseline. Usage above the baseline is billed in arrears at the overage rate. The agreement gives no automatic true-down when headcount falls, so unused seats are still paid for until the next renewal.

**Budget position:** Corvane Systems billed seat overage charges during the current term, and the Relay CRM contract ran 15% over budget. Most of the variance comes from overage. The base licence fee stayed in line with forecast.

Contributing factors identified so far:

- Seasonal and temporary agents were provisioned with named seats and not deprovisioned promptly after their contracts ended.
- Some supervisors and support staff kept seats they no longer use day to day.
- Training cohorts were given live seats instead of sandbox access.
- No one reconciled monthly seat counts against the contracted baseline before invoices arrived.

The finance team has flagged the variance for the quarterly budget review.

## Obligations and Service Levels

Vendor obligations:

- Meet severity-based response and resolution targets for support tickets
- Provide advance notice of planned maintenance
- Supply monthly usage reports showing active seat counts

Kestrel obligations:

- Pay invoices within agreed payment terms
- Keep administrator contacts up to date
- Raise disputes on invoiced charges within the contractual notice period

Service performance over the term has been broadly acceptable. The main issue is commercial, not operational.
