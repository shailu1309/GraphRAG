# Vendor Contract Summary: VoxRoute

**Document owner:** Grace Kim (Contract Manager)
**Audience:** Contact centre leadership, finance team, procurement
**Status:** Internal – not for distribution outside Kestrel Telecom

## Contract Overview

VoxRoute supplies the Switchboard IVR, which handles inbound call routing and self-service menus for the customer contact centre. Grace Kim manages the VoxRoute contract and is the single point of contact for commercial queries, invoice disputes, and change requests. Operational issues (outages, routing faults) should still go through the normal incident process first. Grace should only be copied in where there is a commercial or SLA impact.

The contract covers:

- Hosted IVR platform licence and standard support
- An agreed monthly allowance of inbound call minutes
- Per-minute charges for usage above that allowance
- Standard change requests for menu and prompt updates, up to an agreed number per quarter

Anything outside this list, such as new integrations or additional language packs, is billable as a separate statement of work. It needs sign-off from the contract manager before work starts.

## Financial Position

The VoxRoute contract ran 22% over budget for the period. The overspend came from per-minute overage charges. Licence and support fees came in on budget.

Contributing factors identified in the monthly finance review:

- Call volumes ran higher than forecast during peak billing periods and service disruptions.
- Customers stayed in IVR menus longer than assumed, especially when self-service options failed to resolve their query and they looped back to the main menu.
- The minute allowance was sized on the previous year's volumes. It was not revisited when call patterns changed.
- Overage was reported on invoices in arrears, so the overspend was not visible until well after it had built up.

The finance team has asked for overage to be reported monthly against forecast rather than reconciled quarterly.

## Service Levels and Governance

- **Monthly service review:** Grace Kim and the vendor account manager meet on the second Tuesday of each month. They cover availability, incident summaries, change request status, and usage against allowance.
- **Quarterly commercial review:** This includes a representative from the finance team and a contact centre operations manager. It covers spend to date, a forecast to year end, and any pricing discussions.
- **Availability target:** As set out in the service schedule. Breaches are logged by the contact centre and passed to the contract manager for service credit claims.
- **Invoice checks:** Each invoice is checked against internal call volume reports before approval. Discrepancies over a small tolerance are raised with the vendor within the dispute window.
