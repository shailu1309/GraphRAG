# Vendor Contract Summary: ShiftWise

*Prepared by the contact centre operations team for internal reference. This is a summary only. The signed agreement held by the procurement team takes precedence if anything here conflicts with it.*

## 1. Overview of Supply

ShiftWise supplies Pulse, the workforce scheduling tool used by the contact centre's planning function. Pulse covers the following:

- Agent shift scheduling and rota publication
- Intraday adjustments and break planning
- Volume forecasting (see Section 3 for the forecasting changes)
- Adherence reporting for supervisors

Licences are held under the centre's operations cost centre. Access requests and leaver removals go through the planning team in the usual way. Do not raise them directly with the vendor.

## 2. Key Commercial Terms

| Item | Summary |
|---|---|
| Service | Pulse workforce scheduling tool, hosted by the vendor |
| Licensing basis | Named user licences, reviewed at each renewal |
| Invoicing | Quarterly in arrears, sent to the finance team |
| Support | Vendor service desk, business hours. Priority tickets are logged through the planning team lead |
| Data | Scheduling and forecast data stay the property of Kestrel Telecom. An export is available on exit |
| Renewal notice | Written notice is required before the renewal date. Check the signed agreement for the exact period |

Licence counts drift as headcount changes. The planning team should reconcile active users against the licence total before each quarterly invoice is approved.

## 3. Change: Forecasting Overage Charges

ShiftWise has added overage charges for extra Pulse forecasting runs. Runs above the included allowance are now billed on top of the standard licence fee.

What this means in practice:

- Ad hoc re-forecasts are no longer free, even quick ones done to test a scenario.
- Busy periods carry the most risk. These include outage weeks, price-change letters and seasonal peaks, when planners tend to re-run forecasts several times a day.
- Overage lines appear on the quarterly invoice. The planning team will not see them in advance unless someone tracks usage.

Agreed working approach until the next review:

1. The planning team keeps a simple run log showing the date, who ran it, the reason, and whether it was scheduled or ad hoc.
2. Only the planning team lead or deputy should approve ad hoc runs above the weekly norm.
3. Scenario testing should be batched where possible, rather than run one change at a time.
4. The finance team will flag any invoice where overage exceeds the previous quarter's figure.

## 4. Open Actions

| Action | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm the included forecasting run allowance in writing with the vendor | Procurement team | Next account call |

| Review the last two invoices for any overage already charged | Finance team | End of month |
| Assess whether the current forecasting cadence can be reduced without hurting accuracy | Planning team | Next quarterly review |
| Add overage cost as a standing item on the vendor review agenda | Operations manager | Ongoing |

## 5. Review Arrangements

- **Quarterly:** Vendor account review covering service performance, open tickets, licence count and overage spend. Minutes are filed with procurement.
- **Monthly:** An internal check of the forecasting run log by the planning team, with a short note to the operations manager.
- **Before renewal:** A full assessment of cost and fit, including whether the overage model is still acceptable or should be renegotiated.

Questions about this summary should go to the operations manager in the first instance.
